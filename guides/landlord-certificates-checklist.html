Landlord certificates checklist: how often each one is due

Gas safety every 12 months, EICR every 5 years, EPC every 10 years, smoke and CO alarms and the other checks UK landlords track, with what to give tenants.

Updated 2026-11-06

These are the main safety and compliance records for a let property in England, and how often each is needed. Some depend on the property: a gas certificate only where there is gas, fire risk assessments for common parts and HMOs, and licences only where the council requires one.

The main certificates

RecordHow oftenNotes
Gas Safety Record (CP12)Every 12 monthsBy a Gas Safe registered engineer. Give tenants a copy within 28 days of the check, and new tenants before they move in.
EICR (electrical)At least every 5 yearsGive tenants a copy within 28 days. Remedial work is usually due within 28 days.
EPCValid for 10 yearsNeeded before you market the property. Most lets need a rating of E or better.
Smoke alarmsTest at the start of each tenancyAt least one on every storey with living accommodation.
Carbon monoxide alarmsTest at the start of each tenancyIn any room with a fixed combustion appliance (not gas cookers).
Legionella risk assessmentReview regularlyNo fixed interval; review when something changes.
HMO or selective licenceUsually up to 5 yearsOnly where required by law or by the council.
Fire risk assessmentReview regularlyFor common parts of blocks and for HMOs.

Records many landlords also track

  • Deposit protection certificate and prescribed information (within 30 days of receiving the deposit)
  • Right to Rent checks
  • Landlord or building insurance renewal
  • PAT testing of appliances you supply (good practice)
  • For blocks: fire alarm service, emergency lighting, fire doors, lift examination (LOLER), asbestos re-inspection

How to stay on top of it

Keep one list with each property, each record, the date of the last check and the next due date, and look at what is due in the next 60 days every month.

Official guidance: HSE: gas safety for landlords, GOV.UK: electrical safety standards, GOV.UK: minimum energy efficiency standard. Rules differ in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. This page is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

How often does a landlord need a gas safety certificate?

Every 12 months, by a Gas Safe registered engineer, with a copy to tenants within 28 days.

How often is an EICR needed?

At least every 5 years for private rented homes in England, with a copy to tenants within 28 days.

How long is an EPC valid?

10 years. Most private lets need a rating of E or better.
