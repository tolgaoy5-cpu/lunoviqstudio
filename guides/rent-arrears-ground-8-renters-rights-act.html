Rent arrears and Ground 8 after 1 May 2026 (England)

How the Renters' Rights Act changed Ground 8: the new 3 months or 13 weeks arrears level, the 4-week notice, Universal Credit delays and how to keep records.

Updated 2026-11-05

The Renters' Rights Act changed how landlords in England deal with rent arrears for notices served on or after 1 May 2026. Section 21 "no fault" notices ended on the same date, so the rent arrears grounds under Section 8 matter more than before.

The new Ground 8 level

Ground 8 is the mandatory rent arrears ground: if it is proved, the court must make a possession order. The tenant must owe at least:

  • 3 months' rent if rent is paid monthly, or
  • 13 weeks' rent if rent is paid weekly or fortnightly

The arrears must be at that level both when you serve notice and at the hearing. Before 1 May 2026 the level was 2 months' rent. Only unpaid rent counts, not other charges.

Notice period

For notices served on or after 1 May 2026, the notice period on the rent arrears grounds is at least 4 weeks (it was 2 weeks).

Universal Credit

Arrears that exist only because a Universal Credit payment has not been made yet do not count towards the Ground 8 level. Record Universal Credit payments separately so you can see this.

Below the Ground 8 level

Grounds 10 (some rent unpaid) and 11 (persistent late payment) are discretionary: the court decides whether it is reasonable to make an order. Good records of what was due, what was paid and every contact with the tenant help here.

Good practice before any notice

  1. Contact the tenant early, by phone and in writing.
  2. Send a friendly reminder, then a formal arrears letter with the balance and dates.
  3. Agree a payment plan if you can, and record whether each promise to pay is kept.
  4. Keep a dated log of every call, text, email, letter and visit.

Questions

How much rent must be owed for Ground 8 from 1 May 2026?

At least 3 months' rent if paid monthly, or 13 weeks' rent if paid weekly or fortnightly, both when notice is served and at the hearing.

What is the notice period for rent arrears now?

At least 4 weeks for notices served on or after 1 May 2026 (England).

Do Universal Credit delays count?

No. Arrears caused only by a Universal Credit payment that has not been made yet are not counted towards the Ground 8 level.
