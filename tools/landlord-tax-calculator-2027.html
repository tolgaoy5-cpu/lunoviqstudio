Questions

What are the property income tax rates from April 2027?

24% basic, 44% higher and 49% additional rate in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, under the Budget of 28 October 2026. They still have to pass in the Finance Bill.

Does mortgage interest relief change?

Yes. The Section 24 credit for residential mortgage interest rises from 20% to 24% from 2027/28, so it stays at the property basic rate.

Are landlords with a limited company affected?

Not by these rates. A company pays corporation tax on its profit (19% to 25%), and you pay dividend tax on what you take out.
