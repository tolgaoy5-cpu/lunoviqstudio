About Lunoviq Studio

Lunoviq Studio is run by a UK-based finance professional who works in residential and commercial lettings. The spreadsheets started as tools for real lettings and bookkeeping work: tracking rent and arrears, keeping certificates in date and getting tax figures ready.

How the spreadsheets are made

Each workbook is designed by hand and every formula and rule is checked, including stress tests with the workbook filled to capacity. AI tools help with building and testing. Tax rates, thresholds and rules are checked against GOV.UK and HMRC before each tax year.

What they are not

They are record-keeping and calculation tools, not tax or legal advice, and they do not submit anything to HMRC.

Visit the Etsy shop

Lunoviq Studio makes spreadsheets for UK landlords, letting agents, sole traders and small businesses. Every product works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets unless stated, and is sold as an instant download on Etsy.
